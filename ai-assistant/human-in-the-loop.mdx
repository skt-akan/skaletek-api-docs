{AGENT} reads, gathers and drafts. People decide. This page lists what {AGENT} may do on its own, what always needs a human decision, and where the audit log records each step.

## Why it matters

Your supervisor holds your organization responsible for every rule, every suspicion decision and every report. An examiner may ask who decided and on what basis. With {AGENT}, the answer is always a named person, and the audit log shows what the assistant proposed and who approved it.

## What {AGENT} may do on its own

These actions change nothing in your configuration or your files. {AGENT} does them only within what your own permissions let you see.

| Action | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Read your data | Open the transfers, alerts, screening results and customer profiles you can access |
| Gather | Collect the transfers and facts linked to an alert into a case |
| Explain | Restate an alert explanation in plain language, or explain an obligation, with citations |
| Draft | Write a case summary, a suspicious transaction report (STR) narrative, a rule, a template or sample code |
| Propose | Suggest tasks, a new parameter value, or a link between a rule and an obligation |
| Test | Run a draft rule on past data so you can see what it would have caught |

Every draft carries a visible marker and stays a draft until a person accepts it.

## What always needs a human decision

{AGENT} can prepare each of these. Only a person with the right permission can complete them.

| Decision | Who decides | What {AGENT} prepares |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Activate a rule, or switch it from shadow mode to live | A rule author | The rule, its explanation and the results of testing on past data |
| Accept or dismiss a change package | A reviewer with regulatory permissions | The summary of what changed and the proposed drafts |
| Clear or confirm a screening match | An analyst with screening permissions | A side-by-side comparison of the customer and the list entry |
| Decide whether there is suspicion, and close the case | A compliance officer or money laundering reporting officer (MLRO) | The gathered facts, the tasks and a draft summary |
| Approve and file a report with the authority | The person allowed to sign off filings | A draft narrative and the report fields filled from the case |
| Change users, roles, sign-in or security settings, API keys, or your plan and billing | An administrator | An explanation of how to do it; never the change itself |

Accepting a change package does not activate anything either. It creates draft rules in shadow mode and draft report versions. Someone still activates them in a separate step.

<Important>
  {AGENT} never records a case decision, never files a report and never makes a rule live. If your organization requires a second reviewer for a decision, that rule applies to work {AGENT} prepared too.
</Important>

## Where each action is recorded

<PreviewNotice feature="The audit log" />

The audit log records every step. Entries from {AGENT} are marked as assistant-proposed, so you can tell them apart from work a person wrote.

| What happened | What the audit log shows |
| --- | --- |
| {AGENT} drafted a rule, template or report version | The draft, who asked for it and when |
| A person accepted, edited or rejected a draft | Who did it, when, and the version saved |
| A rule was activated | Who activated it, the version and the time |
| A change package was accepted or dismissed | Who decided, when, and the drafts it created |
| {AGENT} added a summary or tasks to a case | The addition on the case timeline, and who accepted it |
| A case decision was recorded | The person who decided, the outcome and the reason |
| {AGENT} updated your organization profile after you confirmed | The change, the source "assistant" and who confirmed it |

The case timeline and the customer profile show the same history in context. An examiner export of a case (preview) includes it.

## See it in the remittance example

<ExampleNote />

| Step | Done by |
| --- | --- |
| Gather the five transfers to Moussa Ndiaye and the cleared screening match into the case | {AGENT} |
| Propose review tasks and draft a summary citing `SAMPLE-REMIT-0001` to `SAMPLE-REMIT-0005` | {AGENT} |
| Check the citations and edit the summary | The analyst |
| Decide there is suspicion | The MLRO |
| Draft the STR narrative for CENTIF-GN | {AGENT} |
| Review, approve and file the STR (_déclaration de soupçon_) | The MLRO |
| Accept the change package for the fictional 4,000,000 XOF threshold, then activate the new parameter value | An administrator |

Each row appears in the audit log with the person or the assistant who did it.
