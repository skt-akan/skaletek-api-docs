Skaletek helps you know your customers and the businesses you work with, and act when something looks wrong. One customer profile follows each person and organization from onboarding to reporting.

## What do you want to do?

## Set up in four steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get an account">
    [Start a free trial](https://app.skaletek.example/signup?pack=remittance) with sample data loaded (preview), or [book a demo](https://skaletek.example/book-a-demo) to see it with our team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an API key">
    Go to **Settings** › **General Settings** › **Configurations** › **API keys** and click **Create keys**. Give the key only the permissions it needs. See [Authentication](/developers/authentication).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring your data in">
    Send events through the API, as in the [developer quickstart](/developers/quickstart), or upload a spreadsheet with no code (preview). See [Getting data in](/guides/getting-data-in/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work your first alert">
    Open **Alerts**, read the explanation and decide. See the [Alerts guide](/guides/alerts/overview).
  </Step>
</Steps>
