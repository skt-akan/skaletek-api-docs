This track is for people who review activity, investigate and report. Its pages contain no code. They explain what you see in Skaletek, how to decide and how to record your decision.

## Start here

<Card title="Take the 15-minute tour" icon="route" href="/get-started/tour">
  Follow five transfers at a fictional money transfer company, from the first alert to a filed suspicious transaction report (STR).
</Card>

Then read [The 360° customer view](/get-started/360-view). The customer profile is where most of your work begins and ends.

## Who it is for

- **Compliance analysts** who work the alert inbox, review screening matches and work cases.
- **Compliance officers and money laundering reporting officers (MLROs)** who decide suspicion and file reports.
- **Fraud investigators** who explore relationships and follow money between parties.
- **Team leads and managers** who watch workload, deadlines and quality.
- **Procurement and vendor managers** who onboard suppliers and partners with know your business (KYB) checks.

## Top tasks

### Compliance analyst

- Triage the alert inbox and read an alert explanation: [Alerts](/guides/alerts/overview)
- Review and clear a screening match: [Screening](/guides/screening/overview)
- Open and work a case, write findings and close with a rationale: [Cases](/guides/cases/overview)

### Compliance officer or MLRO

- Decide suspicion and export a case for an examiner: [Cases](/guides/cases/overview) (preview)
- Review and file an STR: [Reporting](/guides/reporting/overview)
- Read the traceability matrix and respond to a regulatory change: [Regulatory compliance](/guides/regulatory-compliance/overview) (preview)

### Fraud investigator

- Explore a customer's relationships and follow money between parties: [Customer 360°](/guides/customer-360/overview)
- Bring related alerts together in one case: [Cases](/guides/cases/overview)

### Team lead or manager

- Read dashboards, balance workload and track deadlines: [Dashboards and activity](/guides/dashboards/overview)
- Check alert deadlines and severity: [Alerts](/guides/alerts/overview)

### Procurement or vendor manager

- Start a KYB onboarding, chase missing documents and see what blocks it: [Onboarding](/guides/onboarding/overview)
