Some identity verifications need a person to decide. Skaletek holds them as **Pending review** until a reviewer on your team approves or rejects them. This page shows you how to find them, read the evidence and record your decision.

<Note>
You need the **Review Identity Verifications** permission to take actions. With **View Identity Verifications** only, you can open sessions but not decide.
</Note>

## Before you start

Each identity verification is a **session**. A session waits for review when a check needs a human eye, such as a possible match on a sanctions or politically exposed person (PEP) list, a detail the applicant corrected, or a face match that is not clear. To learn what the applicant does and what Skaletek checks, read [How identity verification works](/guides/onboarding/verifying-people).

## Review a session

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find sessions waiting for review">
    Go to **Verifications** › **Individual**. Set the **Session Status** filter to **Pending review**, or the **Review Status** filter to **Pending**. Use **Date** and **Country** to narrow the list, and **Reload** to refresh it.

    The list has these columns:

    | Column | What it shows |
    | --- | --- |
    | NAME | The applicant's name |
    | KYC CHECKS | Progress of the checks: **Session ongoing**, **Success** with "Cleared all checks", or **Failed** with the step that failed |
    | REVIEW STARTED | When a reviewer first opened the session |
    | REVIEWED | When a reviewer decided |
    | SESSION | Where the session is: **Created**, **In progress**, **Awaiting device switch**, **Pending review**, **Approved**, **Rejected** or **Expired** |
    | REVIEW STATUS | The review decision: **Pending**, **Approved**, **Rejected**, or a dash when no review is needed |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the session">
    Click the applicant's name. The session opens with the person's name and the session status at the top. Opening a session is recorded in its history.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the evidence">
    Work through the sections of the session:

    | Section | What it shows |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Summary** | The applicant, the session status and the overall result |
    | **Identity Document** | The document photos and the details read from them |
    | **Liveness Check** | The selfie and face scan, and whether a live person was present |
    | **Sessions** | The applicant's other verification sessions, newest first |
    | **AML Summary** | Anti-money laundering (AML) screening results for **PEP watchlist**, **Sanctions lists**, **Asset freeze lists** and **Adverse media** |
    | **Session anomaly detection** | Unusual signals in the session that are worth a closer look |

    The tabs give more detail:

    | Tab | What it shows |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Document** | The document images, front and back |
    | **Profile** | The extracted values read from the document next to the provided values the applicant confirmed. A difference means the applicant changed a detail. |
    | **Face match** | The selfie next to the document photo |
    | **Transactions** | The person's transfers and other events |
    | **Device** | The device and connection used for the session |
    | **Location** | Where the session was completed from |
    | **Activities** | Every action on the session, with who did it and when |

    The download icon (**Download evidence bundle**) saves the session's evidence for your records.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take an action">
    Choose the action that fits what you found. The four main actions ask for a reason. Every action is recorded in **Activities** with your name and the time.

    | Action | Use it when | In the dialog |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | **Approve** | The evidence shows the person is who they claim to be. | **Approve Session**: choose a **Reason**, add a **Note**, then **Yes, Approve**. |
    | **Reject** | The evidence shows the identity cannot be accepted. | **Reject Session**: "Leave a note on why this session is being rejected". Choose a **Reason**, add a **Note**, then **Yes, Reject**. |
    | **Request document** | You need another document to decide. | **Request document**: choose the **Document**, a **Reason** and a **Note**, then **Request**. |
    | **Escalate** | A more senior analyst should decide. | **Escalate**: choose the **Analyst**, a **Reason** and a **Note**, then **Escalate**. |

    Three more actions let you correct the session without deciding yet:

    - **Request new liveness** asks for a new selfie and face scan, for example when the face scan was poor.
    - **Change document** replaces the document on the session, for example when the wrong document type was chosen.
    - **Update information** corrects the applicant's details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Important>
Write your reason as if an examiner will read it. The reason and note stay with the session as evidence of why you decided.
</Important>

## After your decision

- **In Skaletek:** the SESSION and REVIEW STATUS columns show **Approved** or **Rejected**. The session shows the decision as a badge. **Escalate** stays available.
- **For the applicant:** while the session waits, the applicant sees **Your verification is under review**, with a note that you will share an update. Tell the applicant the outcome through your own channels. If they open their link again, they see the current result.
- **In your systems:** the decision is recorded as an event, with who decided, and reaches your systems the same way as automatic decisions.

<Accordion title="For developers" icon="code">
  Reviewer decisions reach your systems through the same webhook and status checks as automatic decisions. See [Receiving results](/developers/ekyc/receiving-results) and the [reason codes](/developers/ekyc/reason-codes) that explain each decision.
</Accordion>
