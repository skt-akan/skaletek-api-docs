**In this chapter** (about 10 minutes): you open a case from the alert, and {AGENT} prepares it.
You see what the assistant gathers and proposes, and how you stay in charge of every word that goes into the file.

<ExampleNote />

## What happens in the story

The analyst has read the alert ([chapter 7](/tutorial/an-alert-explained)) and Moussa's profile ([chapter 8](/tutorial/see-the-whole-picture)). Three senders, one agent and 24,000,000 XOF in six days need a proper investigation.

An alert answers "why was this raised?". A **case** answers "what did we investigate, what did we decide and what did we do?". The case is where the work, the evidence and the decision live together.

## Open the case

<PreviewNotice feature="Opening a case from an alert, with a task checklist from a case template," />

From the alert, open a case. The case opens from the _AML suspicion review_ case template, which Teranga Transfer's starter pack provides. The template sets the plan: stages, mandatory tasks and who must check whose work.

The new case, `cas_smp_0001`, names Moussa Ndiaye as its subject and links the alert. Cases are restricted: only people with access to anti-money laundering (AML) investigations can open them.

## The assistant prepares the file

<PreviewNotice feature="Case preparation by the assistant" />

You can prepare a case by hand. When {AGENT} is on, it does the gathering for you in a few seconds. It proposes; it does not decide.

<Steps>
  <Step title="It gathers the linked items">
    {AGENT} collects what belongs in the file and shows it as suggestions:
    - all five transfers to Moussa, including `SAMPLE-REMIT-0004` and `SAMPLE-REMIT-0005`, which arrived after the alert
    - the screening match from [chapter 5](/tutorial/screen-and-clear-a-match), already cleared as a false positive, with its reason
    - profile facts: three senders, cash payouts at Point Marché Tilène, the wallet `SAMPLE-WALLET-0002`
  </Step>
  <Step title="It proposes tasks">
    The template already lists the core tasks, such as reviewing the activity and confirming the typology. {AGENT} suggests extra tasks for this case. For example: "Check whether Fatou Ba and Cheikh Tall have any link to Moussa Ndiaye" and "Review cash payouts at Point Marché Tilène".
  </Step>
  <Step title="It drafts a summary with citations">
    {AGENT} drafts a short case summary. Every sentence links to its source: a transfer, the alert explanation or a profile field. For example: "Moussa Ndiaye received five transfers totalling 24,000,000 XOF in six days [1–5], from three senders [profile], four paid in cash at Point Marché Tilène [1, 3, 4, 5]."
  </Step>
</Steps>

{AGENT} works only from the data in your account that you are allowed to see. It does not invent facts. A sentence it cannot back with a source is flagged, not presented as true.

## You review and approve

Nothing the assistant proposes enters the case until you accept it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Accept the evidence">
    Review the suggested links. Accept the five transfers and the screening match. Each accepted item becomes a link in the case.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the tasks">
    Accept the task about the senders. Assign it to yourself. Dismiss any suggestion you do not need; dismissing is recorded too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the summary">
    Open the draft in the editor. Click a citation to check its source. Reword what you would say differently, then save. The saved summary shows that the assistant drafted it and that you edited and approved it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot name="cases-workspace-assistant-draft" alt="The case on Moussa Ndiaye with the assistant's draft summary open beside the checklist. Each sentence carries a citation, and Accept and Edit buttons sit side by side, so the analyst approves every line." />

The audit log (preview) records each step: what the assistant proposed, what you accepted, edited or dismissed, and when. See [Human in the loop](/ai-assistant/human-in-the-loop) for everything the assistant may and may not do.

## Work the checklist

The case checklist (preview) is where you work. Tasks are grouped by stage, mandatory tasks first. One task is already done: the screening task completes by itself because you cleared Moussa's match in chapter 5.

Some tasks wait on others. Confirming the typology, for example, waits until the activity review and the customer contact are done. That task also needs a second person to review it. One person never both does and approves that work.

<TryIt>
1. Go to **Alerts** and open the alert on **Moussa Ndiaye**.
2. Open a case from the alert (preview).
3. If {AGENT} is enabled for your account, review its suggestions. Accept the five transfers and the screening match.
4. Open the draft summary, check one citation and edit one sentence. Save it.
5. Open the case checklist (preview) and see which task is already done.
</TryIt>

<KeyTakeaway>
The assistant does the gathering and the first draft; you make every call. Each suggestion is cited, and nothing enters the case until a person accepts it.
</KeyTakeaway>
